Cognitive & Affective Computing for Rehabilitation & Embodied Intelligence

CARE stands for Cognitive, Affective, Rehabilitation, and Embodied Intelligence. We develop intelligent technologies for older-adult cognitive health, spanning assessment, affective interaction, VR-based training, and companion robots, while supporting student growth through research.
